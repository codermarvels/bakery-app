Cancellation and refund policy

Last updated: {{LAST_UPDATED}}

Before you pay

Orders that are still "awaiting payment" can be cancelled at any time in the app (Account → My orders → Cancel). No money has been taken, so nothing needs refunding.

After you pay

  • Because our products are freshly prepared food, a paid order can be cancelled only by contacting us before we have prepared it (call {{PHONE}} or email {{EMAIL}} with your order number). If we have not started preparing it, we refund the full amount.
  • Once an order is marked "ready for pickup", it has been prepared for you and cannot be cancelled.
  • If an item is unavailable, or a payment arrives for an order that was already cancelled, we refund the full amount automatically. You do not need to ask.
  • If there is a problem with the quality of an item, tell us at pickup or on the same day and we will replace it or refund it.

How refunds are paid

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method (the same UPI account, card or bank account) through Razorpay.
  • Razorpay sends the refund at once; banks usually show it within 5–7 working days.
  • Payments made at the shop counter (cash or in-person UPI/card) are refunded at the counter.

Not collected

Please collect your order on the day it is ready. Because food is perishable, orders not collected by closing time on that day cannot be refunded, except where we were at fault.
