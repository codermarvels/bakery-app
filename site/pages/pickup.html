How pickup works

  1. Order and pay in the app. Your order shows as Paid.

  3. Come to the shop during opening hours and show your order number in Account → My orders.
  4. We hand over your order and mark it Collected.

Collection time

Please collect your order on the day it is ready, before closing time. See the refund policy for orders that are not collected.
