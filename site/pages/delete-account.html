In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the VSJ BAKERY app and sign in.
  2. Go to Account and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

By email

If you cannot use the app, email {{EMAIL}} from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted immediately: your sign-in (email and password), your name and phone number, and the notes you wrote on orders.
  • Cancelled: orders you have not paid for.
  • Kept without your personal details: records of paid orders and payments (items, amounts, dates and Razorpay references), because Indian tax and accounting law requires us to keep sales records.

If you have a paid order that you have not collected yet, please collect it first (or contact us for a refund); the app will remind you.
