This policy explains what personal data the VSJ BAKERY app collects and how it is used. The data is controlled by {{LEGAL_NAME}}, {{ADDRESS}}. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form), and your phone number if you add it.
  • Orders: what you ordered, prices, pickup notes you write, and order status.
  • Payment records: Razorpay's payment and order reference numbers, the amount and the status. We do not receive or store card numbers, UPI PINs or bank passwords — those go directly to Razorpay.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising ID, and the app has no advertising or tracking.

Why we use it

  • To create your account and let you sign in.
  • To take, confirm and hand over your orders, and to process refunds.
  • To keep sales and tax records that the law requires.
  • To send emails you ask for, such as account confirmation and password reset codes.

Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run the app, which process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — secure database, sign-in and hosting.
  • Razorpay — payments and refunds (Razorpay's privacy policy).

We never sell your data. We share it with authorities only when the law requires.

How long we keep it

  • Your account details are kept until you delete your account.
  • Order and payment records are kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires. When you delete your account they are kept without your name, email or phone number.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules ensure each customer can see only their own orders, and payments are confirmed only by our server, never by the phone alone. Only the bakery's authorised staff can see orders.

Your rights

  • See and correct your details in the app, or ask us for a copy.
  • Delete your account at any time — see Delete account.
  • Complain to our grievance officer (below). If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 18. A parent or guardian should place orders for them.
